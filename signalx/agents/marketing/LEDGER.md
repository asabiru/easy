# Marketing Agent — LEDGER

> Pairs with **Monetization** (MZ-*). Audience splits into two tracks:
>   * **Track A** — HNW investors / family offices for IB capital introduction.
>   * **Track B** — Retail / prosumer traders for auto-trade bot subscription.
> One landing site, two CTAs.

## Mission
Get the right eyeballs on the right CTA at the lowest CAC, and turn each happy user into a public reference.

## Auto-trade bot campaign *(Track B — retail)*

### M-006 · "Sleep through the news. Wake up to wins." *(headline)*
- **Status:** accepted
- **Insight:** retail traders cannot manually beat 1.5-second bot latency on breaking news.
- **Distribution:** hero video on landing + 30-day TikTok / Reels rotation.

### M-007 · 3-stage content funnel
- **Status:** in_progress

**ToFu (free):**
- YouTube/TikTok serial **"Anatomy of a Signal"** — 60-sec format: real tweet → real candle → real trade. **30 videos / mo.**
- Free 7-day email course: "How news moves $NVDA / $TSLA / $XOM perp prices."
- Public **live P&L dashboard** of a real $10k sandbox account on the landing page (radical transparency).

**MoFu:**
- Monthly **public webinar** "Last 30 days inside the bot — every trade, win-rate, latency p50/p95."
- Weekly newsletter: 5 best trades + 2 fakes the bot caught and skipped.
- Twitter Spaces with crypto-trading creators 2× / mo.

**BoFu:**
- 14-day money-back guarantee.
- 7-day paper-trading mode default for every new sub.
- **"Founding 100"** lifetime $49/mo on Manual+ (limited).
- **"First $5,000 traded free"** — no sub fee until client has cleared $5k notional.
