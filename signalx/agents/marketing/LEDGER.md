# Marketing Agent

## Mission
Bring SignalX to the right audience: short-term event-driven crypto-perp
traders, prop desks, and finance Twitter readers. Pair with the Monetization
Agent on every campaign — every funnel must end in a measurable revenue path.

## Active proposals

### M-001 · Positioning: "News-to-trade in under 2 seconds"
Status: in-progress.
Hero claim: SignalX delivers a Telegram alert with action / impact / risk in
under 2 seconds from the moment news breaks on a trusted source. Differentiator
versus generic news bots: built-in market-aware risk filter (spread, liquidity,
late-entry, fake-news) so you don't get spammed with unactionable signals.

### M-002 · Three-layer audience funnel
Status: proposed.
1. **Top of funnel** — a free public Telegram channel with 5–10 redacted
   sample signals per day (no entry / SL / TP). Goal: build credibility,
   collect handles.
2. **Mid funnel** — newsletter (Substack or Beehiiv) covering weekly signal
   recap, win/loss breakdown, top movers, fake-news catches. Lead magnet.
3. **Bottom** — Pro Telegram channel with real-time signals + entry plan +
   tradingview alerts. Paid (see Monetization M-002).

### M-003 · Content engine
Status: proposed.
- **Catch-of-the-week**: each Friday, post one fake/manipulative tweet our
  fake_risk model correctly downgraded. Strong "we save you from rugs" angle.
- **Backtest stories**: "What if you took every NVDA earnings_beat signal in
  the last quarter?" — stitched chart + actual signal_score history.
- **Energy desk**: now that the universe covers XOM/CVX/SHEL/OXY/COP and
  OPEC events, run a parallel "Energy Signals" thread.

### M-004 · Referral
Status: proposed (jointly with Monetization M-005).
Each Pro subscriber gets a referral link; referred conversions earn 30%
commission for 6 months. Crypto exchange affiliates layered on top — every
NVDAUSDT / TSLAUSDT click-through to Bybit/Binance pays a rev-share kickback.

### M-005 · Distribution channels
Status: proposed.
- finance-Twitter community (target 20+ replies / day on relevant news)
- crypto-trading subreddits (/r/algotrading, /r/Bybit, /r/BinanceUS)
- prop-desk Discord servers
- WeChat / Telegram groups in EMEA + APAC
