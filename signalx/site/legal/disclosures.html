1. Who we are

SignalX (the "Service") is a software platform that publishes news-driven trading signals for cryptocurrency-denominated perpetual futures. The Service operates in two distinct modes; which one applies to you depends on which mode you enrol in:

  • Mode A — execution-as-a-service (no custody). You retain full custody of your USDT on a third-party exchange (Bybit, Binance, OKX, etc.) and provide SignalX with a trade-only API key. SignalX never holds, moves, or co-mingles your funds. This is the default mode.
  • Mode B — managed pool (custody). You deposit USDT to a SignalX-controlled treasury wallet. SignalX trades the aggregated pool on your behalf and tracks your fractional ownership through internally-issued shares. Profits and losses propagate proportionally; performance and management fees apply. This mode is currently gated and only available to specific opt-in clients while the Service obtains the necessary licences in your jurisdiction.
  • SignalX is not a broker, dealer, registered investment adviser (RIA), or licensed exchange. In Mode B, SignalX acts as custodian and discretionary manager — see Section 1b below for the full custody disclaimer.
  • SignalX does not execute orders on behalf of clients with respect to regulated securities. Tokenised stock perpetuals are crypto derivatives offered by third-party exchanges; SignalX is not the issuer, market-maker, or settlement agent.

1b. Custody (Mode B) — what holding your USDT means

If you enrol in Mode B (managed pool), you transfer beneficial ownership of your USDT to SignalX. The Service then becomes a custodian and discretionary manager over your funds. You should understand the following:

  • Pooled custody. Your USDT is co-mingled with deposits from other Mode-B clients in shared treasury wallets across one or more chains (TRC20 / TON / Solana / BSC / ERC20). You hold an internal share in the pool — not a segregated balance. The share's value moves with the pool's NAV.
  • No depositor protection. SignalX deposits are not covered by FDIC, SIPC, FSCS, СБВ, or any government depositor-insurance scheme. If SignalX becomes insolvent, suffers a key compromise, or is subject to regulatory seizure, you may lose some or all of your USDT, and you have no claim against any government insurance fund.
  • Smart-contract and operational risk. SignalX uses a combination of self-custodied operator wallets (cold + multi-sig hot) and on-exchange margin accounts. Each layer has a non-zero failure rate. Examples: a treasury private key could be compromised; a partner exchange could halt withdrawals; a cross-chain bridge used to rebalance USDT between networks could be exploited. Any of these can cause a permanent loss of pool capital.
  • Withdrawals are not instant. Withdrawal requests are queued and reviewed by an operator. We may delay, batch, or temporarily pause withdrawals during periods of high volatility, exchange-side restrictions, or anti-money-laundering review of the destination address. You consent to a withdrawal cooldown of up to 24 hours after each deposit and to a 2FA confirmation for withdrawals above 5 000 USDT.
  • Performance & management fees. Mode B charges a 20% performance fee on net gains above your high-water mark (HWM) — a per-client all-time-high share price — and a 2% management fee per year, accrued daily on your equity. Both fees are taken in shares, transferred from your wallet to the SignalX treasury at each NAV snapshot. You owe a performance fee only on gains the pool has actually delivered above your prior HWM, not on the pool's all-time gains.
  • Licensing posture. SignalX may not be licensed as an investment manager, fund operator, virtual-asset service provider (VASP), or collective-investment-scheme manager in your jurisdiction. Where licences are required and not yet held, the Mode-B endpoint will refuse with an HTTP 503 and no deposits will be accepted. By depositing, you confirm that you have read and accepted this section, that you understand SignalX is operating without a financial licence, and that you waive any consumer-protection or depositor-protection claim against SignalX that depends on such a licence existing.
  • Tax. Distributions from Mode B (withdrawals greater than your lifetime deposits) may be taxable as capital gain, ordinary income, or as a return of capital, depending on your residence. SignalX does not issue 1099s, P11Ds, или 2-НДФЛ. You are solely responsible for declaring and paying tax.
  • Right to refuse. SignalX reserves the right to refuse, delay, or unwind any deposit, withdrawal, or share-issuance event at the operator's discretion in response to AML/sanctions screening, fraud investigations, or court order. We will return refused deposits to the originating address minus any chain fees.

If you are not comfortable with any of the above, do not deposit. Use Mode A (no-custody, exchange API keys) instead. Mode A is the safer option from a regulatory / depositor-protection standpoint and is available to all clients.

3. Risk warning

Trading perpetual futures, including tokenised stock perps, carries a high degree of risk and may result in loss of all funds posted as margin and beyond. Specifically:

  • Leverage: small price moves can cause disproportionately large losses. Liquidation can occur within seconds during volatile events.
  • News risk: signal latency between an event and order routing is typically < 2 seconds, but can be longer during exchange outages or wire provider degradation.
  • Fake-news risk: while the Service applies an AI-driven fake_risk score and filters low-confidence events, no filter is perfect. A widely-shared hoax or impersonation account can produce a signal that is later retracted.
  • Counterparty risk: exchanges may freeze accounts, change margining rules without notice, or fail entirely. SignalX has no control over the user's exchange and cannot recover funds frozen or lost there.
  • Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Win-rate, Sharpe, and similar statistics shown on the marketing page are computed from historical SignalX data and may include backfill periods that do not reflect live conditions.
