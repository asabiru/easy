Risk disclosures & legal notices

Last updated: 2026-04-28. Plain-language version follows. The legally binding version is the English text below.

1. Who we are

SignalX (the "Service") is a software platform that publishes news-driven trading signals for cryptocurrency-denominated perpetual futures and, optionally, routes those signals into an exchange account that the user separately operates.

  • SignalX is not a broker, dealer, custodian, money-services business, registered investment adviser (RIA), or licensed exchange.
  • SignalX does not custody, hold, or co-mingle client funds. All trades execute on the user's own account at a third-party exchange (Bybit, Binance, OKX, etc.) using a trade-only API key the user generates.
  • SignalX does not execute orders on behalf of clients with respect to regulated securities. Tokenised stock perpetuals are crypto derivatives offered by third-party exchanges; SignalX is not the issuer, market-maker, or settlement agent.

2. Founder qualification — what it does and does not mean

SignalX's founder holds a qualification certificate as a Russian Federal Financial Markets specialist for: broker activity, dealer activity, securities portfolio management, and forex-dealer activity. This certificate is issued via Plekhanov Russian University of Economics under accreditation by the Bank of Russia.

This personal qualification does NOT mean that SignalX (the company / platform) is licensed, approved, supervised, or otherwise endorsed by the Bank of Russia or any other regulator. The certificate qualifies the named individual only — it does not extend to the entity, the Service, or any product offered through the Service. Any representation that "SignalX is licensed by the Bank of Russia" would be inaccurate and is expressly disclaimed.

3. Risk warning

Trading perpetual futures, including tokenised stock perps, carries a high degree of risk and may result in loss of all funds posted as margin and beyond. Specifically:

  • Leverage: small price moves can cause disproportionately large losses. Liquidation can occur within seconds during volatile events.
  • News risk: signal latency between an event and order routing is typically < 2 seconds, but can be longer during exchange outages or wire provider degradation.
  • Fake-news risk: while the Service applies an AI-driven fake_risk score and filters low-confidence events, no filter is perfect. A widely-shared hoax or impersonation account can produce a signal that is later retracted.
  • Counterparty risk: exchanges may freeze accounts, change margining rules without notice, or fail entirely. SignalX has no control over the user's exchange and cannot recover funds frozen or lost there.
  • Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Win-rate, Sharpe, and similar statistics shown on the marketing page are computed from historical SignalX data and may include backfill periods that do not reflect live conditions.

4. No investment advice

Nothing on the Service constitutes investment, legal, tax, or other professional advice. Signals are informational only. Users are solely responsible for deciding whether to act on a signal. Users should consult a qualified adviser in their jurisdiction before trading derivatives.

5. Geography & sanctions

Access to the Service is restricted to users in jurisdictions where it is lawful to use a non-licensed signal & execution tool for crypto derivatives. The Service is not offered to:

  • Persons or entities ordinarily resident in OFAC-sanctioned countries (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria) and OFAC-sanctioned regions (including Crimea, the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics, Sevastopol).
  • Persons or entities listed on the OFAC SDN list, the EU Consolidated Sanctions list, the UN Sanctions list, or the UK HMT Sanctions list.
  • Persons in jurisdictions that classify crypto perpetual futures as a retail-restricted product without an applicable licensed-broker exemption (e.g. United States retail clients, United Kingdom retail clients post-FCA-PS20/10).
  • Persons on FATF-greylisted source-of-funds risk regions, where Enhanced Due Diligence is required before paid-tier access.

By using the Service, the user represents and warrants that they are not subject to any of the above restrictions. SignalX may, in its sole discretion, decline or suspend any account at any time for sanctions or AML reasons.

6. KYC / AML

Higher-tier features (Auto-Pro, VIP, on-chain vault when launched, payouts in USDT-TON) require identity verification (KYC) by a licensed third-party provider (Sumsub or equivalent). SignalX does not directly receive primary identity documents — those go to the verifier. SignalX retains only the verification verdict and risk flags.

7. Payments

Subscription payments may be charged in USDT-TON via TON Wallet Pay, in fiat via card processor, or by direct bank transfer for Track A clients. All payments are non-refundable except where required by law and except as specifically stated in the 14-day money-back guarantee for first-time subscribers (subject to abuse-detection rules).

8. Referrals

The referral program pays the referrer 20% of the referee's gross revenue for the first 12 months from the referee's first paid invoice. Referral earnings are paid in USDT-TON. SignalX may revoke earnings retroactively in cases of self-referral, sanctions hit on the referrer or referee, fraud, or chargebacks.

9. Privacy

SignalX collects only the data needed to operate the Service: email, exchange API key (encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM), KYC verdict, payment receipts, and trading-activity logs. We do not sell or rent user data. We share data with: (a) the user, (b) regulators upon lawful request, (c) sub-processors listed in the privacy notice (Sumsub for KYC, Sentry for error tracking, Fly.io for hosting, OpenAI/Anthropic for some signal-classification components — only event metadata, never personal data, is sent there).
