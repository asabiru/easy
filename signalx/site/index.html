How it works

Three steps. Two seconds. No custody.

01 / Detect

Multi-source ingest

Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, CNBC, SEC EDGAR, EIA, OPEC, X (Twitter), StockTwits, Reddit. Cross-source confirmation in a 90-second window boosts confidence and lowers fake-risk.

02 / Score

Anti-fake engine

Each event gets a fake_risk 0–100. Linguistic markers (PUMP / RUMOR / LEAKED), account reputation, pre-news price action and source reliability all feed in. ≥50 = SKIP.

Your account, our edge

Hard rule #2

FAQ

Common questions

How does the bot trade without holding my money?

You generate a trade-only API key on your exchange (no withdraw, IP-whitelisted to our servers) and paste it on subscribe. The bot calls create_order on your account — that's it. We never have a withdraw permission, ever.

What's "paper mode"?

For the first 7 days every new subscription is in paper mode — you see the trades the bot would have made with a simulated $10k balance. No real orders. After that you choose to flip live.

What if the news is fake?

Each event is scored 0-100 for fake-risk. Linguistic markers, account age, follower count, presence of an authoritative link, pre-news price action and cross-source confirmation all factor in. Score ≥ 50 ⇒ the bot SKIPs entirely.

Which exchanges do you support?

Bybit, Binance, OKX, Backpack, Aster, Hyperliquid (in roll-out). The bot uses ccxt under the hood, so adding a new venue is a config change, not a rewrite.

Is this regulated?

SignalX is a signal & execution-tooling provider — we're not your broker, custodian, or RIA. For Track A managed accounts we partner with white-label RIAs. We never market guaranteed returns.
